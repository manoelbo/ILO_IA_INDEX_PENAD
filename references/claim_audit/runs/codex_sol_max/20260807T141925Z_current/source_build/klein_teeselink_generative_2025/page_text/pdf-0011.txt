3    Methodology
We use a difference-in-differences design with continuous treatment intensity to esti-
mate the effect of LLM exposure on UK labor market outcomes. Our identification
strategy exploits variation in occupational and firm-level exposure to LLM capabili-
ties, as measured by Eloundou et al. (2024), combined with the timing of ChatGPT’s
public release in November 2022. We conduct three types of analyses: firm-level em-
ployment stocks using LinkedIn profile data, firm-level hiring decisions based on job
posting data, and occupation-level hiring patterns using job posting data.
    For our firm-level employment stock analysis, we estimate the following specifi-
cation:
                         X30

where Yit represents outcomes for firm i in month t, including log total employment,
log employment by seniority level (junior vs. senior), the fraction of low-seniority
positions, and average compensation. Exposurei is the firm’s pre-treatment LLM
exposure score, calculated as the employment-weighted average of occupational ex-
posure scores using the workforce composition on 30 October. We standardize the
exposure coefficients across all analyses to make the coefficients more easily inter-
pretable. The term 1[t=τ ] denotes indicators for event time τ relative to November
2022, with October 2022 (τ = −1) as the reference period. We include firm fixed
effects γi to control for time-invariant firm characteristics, and industry-by-period
fixed effects δjt to account for sector-specific shocks that might correlate with tech-
nology adoption patterns. Standard errors are clustered at the firm level.
    This event study design allows us to test for parallel pre-treatment trends, as
well as to examine the evolution of treatment effects over time, showing when and
how labor market impacts start to emerge, and whether they persist, intensify, or
attenuate over time. We use 15 months of pre-treatment data and 30 months of
post-treatment data. To summarize the overall treatment effect, we also report
average post-treatment effects from a static specification:

                     Yit = β · Exposurei × Postt + γi + δjt + ϵit                  (4)

where Postt is an indicator for periods after November 2022.
