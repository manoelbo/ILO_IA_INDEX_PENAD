This analysis examines occupation-level job posting data to understand how ag-

LLM exposure across 384 occupations, from minimally affected roles such as cooks
(exposure score: 0) to highly exposed positions like proofreaders and copy markers
(exposure score: 0.86). We examine three outcomes: posting volumes to measure
aggregate demand, advertised salaries to assess compensation responses, and within-
occupation salary dispersion to measure wage polarization.

our identification strategy. Following ChatGPT’s release, however, exposed occupa-

statistically significant after 7 months and intensifying thereafter. After 20 months,
we see a slight resurgence, and job postings converge back to their original levels.
