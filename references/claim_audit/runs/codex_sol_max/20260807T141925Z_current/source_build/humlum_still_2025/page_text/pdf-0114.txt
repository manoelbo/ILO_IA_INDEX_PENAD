G.1        Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

As a robustness check, we estimate workplace rates of encouragement using Empirical
Bayes shrinkage with a Beta-Binomial model; see Walters (2024) for a detailed introduction
to Empirical Bayes methods. The shrinkage is performed separately for each occupation,
allowing underlying encouragement rates to vary systematically across occupations.
