A.2.3    Equilibrium

An equilibrium consists of encouragement policies {Ej }, skill prices {Wj }, worker-firm
allocations {Ij }, and adoption decisions {Aij }, such that firms and workers optimize
(Equations (16), (17), (21), and (23)) and markets clear (Equations (14) and (15)).
Without chatbots, all firms are symmetric and each employs an equal share of baseline
human capital.


Sorting. When chatbots are available, the equilibrium features full segregation: assuming
α̂1 ≥ 0, all adopters sort into encouraged firms and all non-adopters into non-encouraged
firms, or both. We focus on the empirically relevant case where some but not all firms
encourage and some but not all workers adopt.
