A.5      Theoretical Framework: Automation Tomorrow, Hiring Reductions
         Today

This section develops a parsimonious framework in which expectations of future labor-
saving productivity gains depress current hiring when employment is costly to adjust. We
first present a two-period model that delivers an explicit “firing wedge” in the period-0
hiring condition. We then embed the same mechanism in a task-based production struc-
ture in the spirit of Acemoglu and Restrepo (2019).
