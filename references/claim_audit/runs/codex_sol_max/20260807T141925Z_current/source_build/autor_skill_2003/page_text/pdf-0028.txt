IV.C. Employing Contemporaneous Measures of Computer
      and Capital Investment
     A limitation of the CPS computer measure used so far is that
it is only available for the 1980s and 1990s. To provide more
comprehensive measures of computer and capital investment
available for the entire 1959 –1998 period, we draw on the Na-

government [U. S. Department of Commerce 2002a, 2002b]. As a

investment in computer hardware, software and peripherals per
