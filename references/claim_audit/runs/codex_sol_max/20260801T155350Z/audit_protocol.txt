VERDICT TAXONOMY

Fill the existing fact_checked field with exactly one of:

- SUPPORTED
- PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- OVERSTATED
- CONTRADICTED
- NOT_FOUND
- NOT_VERIFIABLE
- SOURCE_BLOCKED

Definitions:

SUPPORTED:
The cited work supports the full claim with compatible population, geography, period, direction, magnitude, comparison, method, and modality.

PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED:
The claim contains independently meaningful components and the source supports only some of them.

OVERSTATED:
The source points in the same general direction, but the dissertation strengthens causality, magnitude, scope, certainty, generality, or interpretation.

CONTRADICTED:
The source reports a materially opposing result.

NOT_FOUND:
No supporting evidence was found after a documented, sufficiently exhaustive search of the cited work.

NOT_VERIFIABLE:
The statement is normative, interpretive, or an author inference that cannot be established from the cited source alone.

SOURCE_BLOCKED:
The source is missing, corrupt, incorrect, inaccessible, or too version-incompatible to evaluate responsibly.

Only SUPPORTED is a full pass. Every other verdict must produce a structured issue or manual-review record.

AUTHOR_INFERENCE RULE

For AUTHOR_INFERENCE rows, distinguish:

Do not claim that an article explicitly states an author inference merely because the inference appears reasonable.
