  In the case of trade data, however, this estimator may have an important drawback.
Trade data for larger countries (as gauged by GDP per capita) tend to be of higher quality
(see Frankel and Wei, 1993, and Frankel 1997); hence, models assuming that V [yi |x] is

prone to contamination by measurement error and less informative about the curvature
of E [yi |x].
