    The top pair of bars in Figure 2 refers to overall adoption for the two technologies two
to three years after the introduction of the first mass-market product. The bar for the RPS
repeats the 45% overall adoption rate for genAI reported in Figure 1a. Overall PC adoption is
roughly 20% in 1984, implying that genAI has been adopted at a faster pace than the PC. As
another more recent comparison, in 1995 the internet was opened to commercial traffic after
the decommissioning of NSFNet (Leiner et al., 2009).9 Two years later, 21% of US adults
reported access to the internet according to the International Telecommunication Union, which
compiles subscriber data from national regulatory bodies and service providers (Pena-Lopez
et al., 2009). This is a similar pace of overall adoption to PCs, and substantially slower than
the adoption of genAI.
