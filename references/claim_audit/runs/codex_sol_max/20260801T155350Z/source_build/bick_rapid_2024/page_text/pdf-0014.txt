On average, genAI use is associated with a weekly earnings premium of 20 log points, a wage
premium of 15 log points, and 5 log points for weekly hours. However, these average effects
mask important heterogeneity. The coefficients on earnings, wages, and hours are all increasing
in genAI use intensity, and infrequent users show no statistical difference between non-users.
