    Figure 9a plots genAI use against time savings at the industry level using RPS data. We
observe considerable variation in genAI use across industries, which is strongly correlated with
genAI time savings. Information Services and Finance have the highest genAI use (9.4% and
9.6%, respectively) and time savings (2.7% and 2.3%, respectively), while Accommodation and
Food Services has the lowest use (1.5%) and time savings (0.6%). The slope of the dashed OLS
line is 0.21, indicating that a 10 percentage point increase in genAI use is associated with a
2.1% rise in time savings. The relationship is approximately linear; in particular, we do not
observe a plateauing of time savings at higher rates of adoption.
