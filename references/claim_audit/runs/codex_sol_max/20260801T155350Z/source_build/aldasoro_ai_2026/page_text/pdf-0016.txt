    The heterogeneity in AI development and adoption between the US and EU is at the
core of our empirical approach. By leveraging detailed survey and financial data, our
analysis aims to capture the exogenous component of AI adoption for EU firms, using
data on similar firms in the US. This approach helps mitigate endogeneity bias in the
subsequent regression analysis, which may arise if AI adoption is correlated with unob-
served firm-specific factors, such as managerial quality, innovation culture or strategic
priorities. By using AI adoption patterns in the US – where firms operate under different
