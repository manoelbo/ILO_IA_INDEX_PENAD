3.7     Invalidity of pretrend tests based on pre-period coefficients.

Contamination undermines the practice of testing for pretrends using pre-period coefficients. Proposition 3

implies that when effects are not homogenous across cohorts, it is problematic to interpret non-zero estimates

for µg as evidence for pretrends, where the set g contains some leads ` < 0. Proposition 4 implies that even

with homogeneous treatment effect, if the effects associated with the excluded periods are not zero, then

contamination may still occur. Therefore without strong assumptions, pre-period coefficients should not be

used to test for pretrends because contamination can lead to estimates that are non-zero in the absence of

pretrends or zero in the presence of pre-trends.
