I.A. The Demand for Routine and Nonroutine Tasks
    The informal task framework above implies three postulates
about how computer capital interacts with human labor input.
    A1. Computer capital is more substitutable for human labor
         in carrying out routine tasks than nonroutine tasks.
    A2. Routine and nonroutine tasks are themselves imperfect
         substitutes.
    A3. Greater intensity of routine inputs increases the mar-
         ginal productivity of nonroutine inputs.
