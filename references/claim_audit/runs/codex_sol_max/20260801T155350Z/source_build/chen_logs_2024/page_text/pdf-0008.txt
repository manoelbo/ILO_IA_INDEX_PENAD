2.2     Additional remarks and extensions
Remark 1 (ATEs for logpc ` Y q). In some settings, researchers consider the ATE for logpc ` Y q
and investigate sensitivity to the parameter c. Observe that logp1 ` aY q “ logpap1{a ` Y qq “
logpaq ` logp1{a ` Y q, and thus the ATE for logp1 ` aY q is equal to the ATE for logp1{a ` Y q.
Hence, varying the constant term for logpc ` Y q is equivalent to varying the scaling of the outcome
when using mpyq “ logp1 ` yq. Proposition 1 thus implies that if treatment affects the extensive
margin, one can obtain any desired magnitude for the ATE for logpc ` Y q via the choice of c. In
particular, the ATE for logpc ` Y q grows large in magnitude as c Ñ 0, and small as c Ñ 8.
