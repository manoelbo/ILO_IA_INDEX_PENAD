A.3.2    Heterogeneous Encouragement Costs

For analytical simplicity, our main analysis focused on the case where firms face the same

encouragement cost, κj = κ for all j. In this section, we consider the general case with

heterogeneous encouragement costs, letting Fκ denote the CDF of employer encouragement

in terms of the relative skill price, W1:0 = W1 /W0 , and then combine supply and demand
