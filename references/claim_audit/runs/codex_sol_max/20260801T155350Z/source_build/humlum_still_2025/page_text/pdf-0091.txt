consistent with our 2023 survey round, in which women were significantly more likely to

cite lack of training as a barrier to using ChatGPT (Humlum and Vestergaard, 2025).

       More broadly, the role of employer initiatives in AI chatbot adoption echoes a large

literature on the diffusion of general-purpose technologies, which highlights the need for

complementary investments and coinvention (Bresnahan and Greenstein, 1996; David,

1990; Feigenbaum and Gross, 2024). The fact that initiatives can be counterproductive in

isolation, yet complementary in combination, aligns with the concept of “Productivity

J-curves,” where firm-based complementary investments are needed to unlock the potential

of new technologies (Brynjolfsson, Rock and Syverson, 2021; David, 1990).
