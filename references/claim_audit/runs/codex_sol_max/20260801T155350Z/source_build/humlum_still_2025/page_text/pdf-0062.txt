We may use the policy rules for encouragement, adoption, and sorting to further bound

the equilibrium skill price differentials.

   First, in any equilibrium where some but not all firms encourage chatbot use, Equation

(19) implies that the relative skill price must lie within:


                           log W1:0 ∈ [κmax /(1 − ϵ), κmin /(1 − ϵ)] .                  (67)


Similarly, in any equilibrium where only some workers sort into encouraged firms, Equation

(22) implies that:


                                     log W1:0 ∈ [−α̂1 , 0] .                            (68)


That is, when log W1:0 > 0, even non-adopters prefer encouraged firms; when log W1:0 <

−α̂1 , even adopters prefer non-encouraged firms.
