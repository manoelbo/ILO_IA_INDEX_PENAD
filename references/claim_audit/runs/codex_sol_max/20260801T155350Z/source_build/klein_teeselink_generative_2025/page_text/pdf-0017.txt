    Figure 4 presents event study estimates for the probability that exposed vs.
less exposed firms post any job vacancy in a given month. The results show a
change in hiring patterns following November 2022. Prior to this date, firms with
different exposure levels did not have statistically significantly different trends in
job postings.8 Following the release of ChatGPT, however, we observe that a one
standard deviation increase in LLM exposure reduces firms’ likelihood of posting new
jobs by 1.0 percentage point (p < 0.001, see Table A1). This effect emerges gradually
and becomes statistically significant after approximately 10 months, mirroring the
timeline of employment stock adjustments.
