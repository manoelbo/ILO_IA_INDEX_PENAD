    Table 1 presents firm-level employment summary statistics. Firm-level employ-
ment stocks increased for both exposure groups following ChatGPT’s introduction,
rising 11.2 percent for low-exposure firms (from 456 to 507 employees) and 10.8
percent for high-exposure firms (from 639 to 708 employees). However, this increase
could reflect an increase in the number of people joining LinkedIn during the sam-
ple period. The workforce composition reveals important differences: high-exposure
firms maintain a more senior-skewed workforce, with 33.3 percent of positions at
high-seniority levels compared to 29.5 percent in low-exposure firms. Following
ChatGPT’s release, this seniority gap widened slightly, as the share of high-seniority
positions increased by 0.5 percentage points in high-exposure firms versus 0.2 per-
centage points in low-exposure firms. These patterns suggest that high-exposure
firms are gradually shifting their workforce composition toward more senior roles,
potentially reflecting the displacement of entry-level positions. Consistent with their
more senior workforce composition, high-exposure firms offer higher average com-
pensation.
