    Table 2 presents summary statistics for occupation-level job postings. We sep-
arate the data by high vs. low exposure occupations, and show the data for the
periods before and after the release of ChatGPT in November 2022. Job posting
volumes declined across both exposure groups following the introduction of Chat-
GPT, with high-exposure occupations experiencing a sharper decline of 17.9 percent
(from 3,343 to 2,745 monthly postings) compared to 17.2 percent for low-exposure
occupations (from 4,056 to 3,358). Second, average posted salaries increased for both
groups, rising from £44,828 to £49,360 (10.1 percent) in low-exposure occupations
and from £46,177 to £50,263 (8.8 percent) in high-exposure occupations, with salary
dispersion increasing similarly across both groups. Finally, high-exposure occupa-
tions have LLM exposure scores approximately 6 times higher than low-exposure
occupations.
