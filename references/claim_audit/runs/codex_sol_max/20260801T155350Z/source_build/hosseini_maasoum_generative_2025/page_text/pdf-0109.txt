A.28      The Junior Hiring Decline—Heterogeneity by Sector

This appendix examines heterogeneity in the decline in junior hiring by sector. For this, we re-estimate
Equation 15 for junior hires separately by sector. Results are presented in Figure A.30. Across all sectors,
adopting firms exhibit a sharp and statistically significant relative decline in junior hiring after 2023Q1,
while senior hiring remains stable or increases slightly. This pattern indicates that the contraction in junior
hiring is broad-based across industries and not driven by any single sector disproportionately reducing
demand for junior workers.
