tion within firms. Finally, because GenAI is still in the early stages of diffusion, longer-run
adjustments in training, task allocation, wages, and internal career ladders may either at-
tenuate or amplify the effects documented here.
   Even with these caveats, the results suggest that GenAI adoption may be shifting labor
demand away from entry-level tasks and narrowing the lower rungs of internal career
ladders. Because early-career jobs are central to skill formation and lifetime earnings
growth, such changes may have consequences beyond short-run employment. Whether
these patterns persist, and whether firms respond by redesigning training, tasks, or career
progression, remains an important question for future research.
